**Notes:**
- Data compiled as of September 2026 from official guidelines, PIB releases, and state portals.
- “Ongoing” means the scheme continues without a fixed end date (subject to annual budgetary allocations and periodic extensions).
- Special Category under PMEGP automatically includes women + NER, Hill & Border areas, and Aspirational Districts.
- State schemes have strict domicile requirements; central schemes are available nationwide with regional incentives as noted.
- Always verify latest eligibility, interest rates, and application processes on the respective official portals before applying.
